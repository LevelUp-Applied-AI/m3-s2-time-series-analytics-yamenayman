# Executive Report: E-commerce Performance Analysis (Last 12 Months)

## 1. Revenue Trends
Over the past 12 months, our business has shown significant variations in revenue generation. 
* **Growth Peaks:** The highest Month-over-Month (MoM) growth occurred in November and December 2025, reaching 50.9% and 43.0% respectively. This indicates a very strong holiday/end-of-year shopping season.
* **Anomalies:** We experienced a severe drop in revenue in January 2026 (-57.31% MoM). This post-holiday slump is extreme and requires immediate marketing intervention in future cycles.
* **Trend Smoothing:** The 30-day moving average clearly shows a smooth, continuous upward trend from mid-2025 until the end of the year, confirming that the business was consistently scaling before the January drop.

## 2. Customer Retention (Cohort Analysis)
Tracking our cohorts by their first-purchase month reveals our actual customer lifetime value potential.
* **Strongest Cohorts:** The Q4 cohorts (October, November, and December 2025) showed exceptional retention, with the December cohort hitting a massive 93.04% retention rate at 90 days.
* **Weakest Cohorts:** The mid-year cohorts (e.g., July 2025) struggled early on, with only a 35% retention rate in the first 30 days. 
* **Insight:** Customers acquired during high-traffic holiday seasons tend to be much more loyal or are incentivized by recurring end-of-year promotions.

## 3. Category Performance
By combining revenue share and growth window functions, we identified our core revenue drivers.
* Our analysis shows that certain categories naturally dominate the revenue share (consistently holding top percentages).
* However, tracking MoM growth per category reveals that even smaller categories occasionally experience sudden growth spikes, indicating shifting consumer interests or successful niche marketing campaigns.

## 4. Recommendations for Next Quarter
Based on the data, I recommend the following actions for the business:
1. **Prepare for the January Slump:** The -57% drop in January must be mitigated next year. I recommend launching a "New Year, New You" aggressive retention campaign starting December 26th to carry momentum into January.
2. **Replicate Q4 Acquisition Strategy:** Since the Nov/Dec cohorts retain at over 90%, we should analyze exactly what acquisition channels were used during those months and reallocate budget to them year-round.
3. **Optimize Re-engagement Window:** Since cohorts like July show weak 30-day retention, implement automated email/discount triggers on Day 20 for mid-year customers to encourage that crucial second purchase.
